Drop your .glb 3D models into this folder — Arena3D.tsx auto-loads them on refresh!

Expected filenames (all optional; missing files fall back to the procedural cel-shaded rigs):
- jinwoo.glb          -> Sung Jinwoo / main hero (auto-scaled to 2.05m, cel-shaded)
- igris.glb           -> Igris Shadow Commander (auto-scaled to 2.2m, obsidian-crimson shadow tint)
- shadow_soldier.glb  -> Shadow Infantry / summons (auto-scaled to 1.75m, obsidian-violet shadow tint)
- mob.glb             -> Regular dungeon wave monster (auto-scaled to 1.95m, cel-shaded)
- boss.glb            -> Gate Boss (auto-scaled to 2.8m, cel-shaded)

Tip: If your .glb has embedded Mixamo animations named "Idle" and "Attack"/"Slash",
AnimationMixer plays them automatically during idle stance and combat hits.
